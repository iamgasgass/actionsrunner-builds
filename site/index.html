What it installs

Pick what goes on your iPhone

iLoader X downloads the app from the project that makes it, signs it with your Apple ID and installs it.

SideStore

An app store for sideloaded apps. It installs them and keeps them refreshed, all on the device.

LiveContainer + SideStore

Runs your apps inside one container, so they don't count toward a free Apple ID's three-app limit. SideStore is built in.

AltStore

AltStore Classic, with the pairing file already in place for its Remote AltServer.

Madeira

Runs Windows PC games on iPhone and iPad.

Custom .ipa

Any .ipa of your own, picked from Files or pasted as a link.

How it works

One run, start to finish

No cable and no desktop tool. What a computer used to do is a few taps in the app.

  1. Connect LocalDevVPN

    A free app from the App Store that gives the iPhone a tunnel back to itself. That tunnel is how iLoader X reaches the device without a computer.

  2. Sign in with your Apple ID

    It signs the app the way Apple signs one for a developer. Your details stay on the iPhone and are used only to sign and install.

  3. Tap Install

    On iOS 27 the iPhone pairs with itself: it shows a code, you type it into the prompt in Settings, and the run carries on. From iOS 17.4 you import a pairing file once.

  4. Open your new app

    The app lands on the Home Screen, with the pairing file already handed over, ready to sideload on its own.

Privacy

Your Apple ID stays on your iPhone

Stored in the keychain

The password is kept in this iPhone's keychain and nowhere else. Nothing is sent to iamgasgass.

Straight to Apple

Sign-in goes to Apple directly, through the anisette server you pick in Settings.

Open source

Every line is on GitHub, so you can read exactly what runs on your iPhone.

Tools

Tools for after the install

Everything a computer used to be needed for, under the Tools tab.

Side by Side

Installs iLoader X, SideStore or any IPA on a friend's iPhone over Wi-Fi.

Pairing

Makes, exports and hands the pairing file to SideStore, StikDebug and other apps.

Certificates

Shares one certificate with SideStore, so a free Apple ID never revokes the other's.

JIT

Turns JIT on for development-signed apps, emulators included.

Sources

AltStore-style sources, with updates and release notes.

Location spoofing

Moves the iPhone's location anywhere, or plays a route.

Sideloaded apps

Sees when each app expires and refreshes them in one go.

Entitlements

Shows what an app is allowed to do, and what your Apple ID can grant.
